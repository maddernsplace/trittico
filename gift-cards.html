Gift Card FAQs

When will the recipient receive their gift card?

Gift cards are emailed within 24 hours of purchase. For urgent gifts, call us on (08) 8381 3446.

Do gift cards expire?

No — our gift cards have no expiry date and can be used any time.

Can I buy a gift card in-store?

Yes! Visit us at 154 Main South Rd, Hackham, or call (08) 8381 3446 to arrange a physical gift card.

What if I spend less than the gift card value?

Any remaining balance stays on the card for your next visit.
