Kitchen Printer / Order Notifications
How to set up automatic kitchen printing When a customer pays, an email is automatically sent to your kitchen printer or email.

Step 1 — EmailJS (free): Sign up at emailjs.com, add your Gmail/Outlook as a service, then create an email template with these variables:
{{order_id}} • {{order_type}} • {{order_time}} • {{{docket_html}}} (triple braces for HTML) • {{items_text}} • {{order_total}} • {{order_note}}

Step 2 — Kitchen email: Set the "To email" below to your kitchen computer email, or your Star Micronics printer's cloud email address (from Star Cloud Services) for fully automatic printing — no one needs to click anything.

Step 3 — Stripe redirect: In your Stripe dashboard → Payment Link → After Payment → set redirect URL to https://maddernsplace.github.io/trittico/order-complete.html
