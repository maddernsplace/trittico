How It Began

A Dream from Naples

Trittico Ristorante was founded with a single dream: to bring the true taste of Italy to Adelaide’s south. The name Trittico — Italian for “triptych” — reflects our founding philosophy: three pillars that guide everything we do.

Located at 154 Main South Road in Hackham, we opened our doors with a small but passionate team, a wood-fired oven shipped from Naples, and recipes perfected over decades. The response from our community was overwhelming.

Today, Trittico is proudly rated 4.8★ across 439+ reviews — a testament to our commitment to authenticity, quality, and the kind of service that makes every guest feel at home.

What Drives Us

Our chefs trained in Italy and bring authentic culinary tradition to every dish. From hand-rolled pasta to 48-hour fermented pizza dough, we never cut corners.
