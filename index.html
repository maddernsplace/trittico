Our Story

Benvenuti alla
Famiglia Trittico

Trittico Ristorante was born from a deep love of Italian food. Our name — Trittico, meaning “triptych” in Italian — represents the three pillars of our kitchen: quality ingredients, traditional technique, and heartfelt hospitality.

Located at 154 Main South Road, Hackham, we’ve been proudly serving Adelaide’s south with authentic Italian flavours. Every dish tells a story of Italy.
